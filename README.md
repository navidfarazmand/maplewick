# Maplewick

A focus game prototype: you are the mayor of a run-down town, and your real focus time (phone away) builds it back up. Gold comes from taxes, focus powers your builder, and the play time you earn is your break.

Play it on a phone at **https://navidfarazmand.github.io/maplewick/** and add it to your home screen (Safari: Share, then Add to Home Screen).

- `index.html` is the whole game (one file).
- `manifest.webmanifest`, the icons and `sw.js` make it work as a home-screen app, also offline.

Progress is saved on the device, in the browser.
