The loop

Focus is the gate. Play is the break.

  1. Pick a length. Anything from 5 minutes to 2 hours. A 10-second countdown lets you settle in, and you can still back out for free.
  2. Put the phone away. Sam the builder works on the town while you work on your thing.
  3. Collect. Every session pays play time, gold and XP, and ends with a chest: wooden, silver, gold or royal.
  4. Take your break. Spend the play time building and looking after the town. When it runs out, the break is over.

Made for wandering minds

Small starts. Breaks with edges.

Five minutes counts

Short sessions earn their share. Start again within 20 minutes and a combo builds, up to double rewards.

A break that ends

You can bank at most 45 minutes of play and earn 2 hours a day, so the game never swallows the evening.

Every problem is a button

Trash on the street, a fire, a sick neighbour: tap it and the fix is right there.

Quiet while you work

Fires, break-ins and storms only happen while you play. Nothing goes wrong in town while you focus.

The town

A town that keeps living.

Days last 24 minutes, with weather and seasons. Firefighters, police, nurses, sweepers and gardeners each work from their own building and only reach so far, so where you build matters.
